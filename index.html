Assumptions & safety notes
  • This tool is decision support only. It does not replace POH/AFM performance data or pilot judgment.
  • Runway length is calculated from runway-end coordinates. Field elevation is estimated from runway-end elevations (median).
  • Wind and runway headings are treated as true. No magnetic correction is applied.
  • Monitoring is GPS-based; update rates vary by device and conditions. Keep the app in the foreground.
